Summary
  • You don't need a phone number to launch. SmartCallAI now has a voice call inside the web app at /call. It uses the same AI agent as the phone line and costs only OpenAI usage: speech-to-text is $0.003 per minute, plus a little for the voice and the model.
  • Twilio's free trial fits our design. It has no card requirement, 75 voice minutes and 30 days. Speech recognition (<Gather>) and <Say> are included, and those are exactly what our new Twilio routes use. Recording, audio streaming and ConversationRelay are blocked on trial.
  • The Twilio trial has two limits. Only verified phone numbers can call, up to 5. Calls are limited to your sign-up country, so a Kenyan account calling a US trial number may be refused.
  • Browser calling through Twilio needs no number at all ($0.004/min). Twilio doesn't say whether it works on a trial, so we'll find out when you sign up.
  • LiveKit, Vapi and ElevenLabs give a free US number or free minutes. Each would replace our AI SDK agent with their own, so they don't fit what you asked for.

What Twilio's free trial allows

From Twilio's trial docs, last updated 23 Sep 2026:

  • Free units: 75 call minutes, 100 SMS, and text-to-speech and speech recognition marked "Included". No credit card, and the trial ends 30 days after sign-up.
  • Who can call: only verified numbers, up to 5, and your own number is verified at sign-up. Inbound calls to a trial number must also come from verified numbers.
  • Where: "SMS messages and voice calls are restricted to your sign-up country." Up to 5 calls at once.
  • Allowed on trial: <Say>, <Gather>, <Play>, <Hangup>, <Redirect>.
  • Blocked on trial: <Record>, <Stream>, <ConversationRelay>, and <Dial> to a phone number. On trial, a hand-off to a human agent will say "connecting you" but can't ring them.
  • After upgrading (pay as you go): US number $1.15/mo, inbound $0.0085/min, <Gather> speech $0.02 per use, ConversationRelay $0.07/min, browser calls $0.004/min. A Kenyan Twilio number is $16/mo, and calls to Kenya are about $0.38–0.39/min.

Older Help Center pages and blogs still describe a "$15 trial credit". The current docs page lists free units instead, and I went with the current page.

What you need to do

  1. Now, free: share /call. Once the app is deployed, anyone with a browser and a microphone can talk to the AI.
  2. Try a real phone call with Twilio:
    1. Create the account yourself at twilio.com; no card is needed.
    2. Put TWILIO_AUTH_TOKEN and PUBLIC_BASE_URL in .env.local.
    3. Expose the app with a tunnel: cloudflared tunnel --url http://localhost:3100.
    4. On the trial number, set "A call comes in" to POST https://<tunnel>/api/voice/twilio and "Call status changes" to /api/voice/twilio/status.
    5. Call it from your verified phone.
  3. If Kenya-to-US calling is refused on trial: try browser calling through Twilio. Create an API key and a TwiML App pointing at /api/voice/twilio, then set the four TWILIO_* browser variables. /api/voice/twilio/token already issues the access token. The browser button is the next thing to build once your account exists, because I couldn't test it without one.
  4. Later, with budget: a Kenyan number from Africa's Talking (already built), or upgrade Twilio. The AI agent code doesn't change either way.

Unknowns

  • Whether Twilio browser calls work on a trial account. Twilio doesn't say; the only way to know is to try.
  • Whether a Kenyan-registered trial can call its assigned trial number. The country rule suggests not.
  • The trial announcement message older docs mention doesn't appear on current pages.
  • Plivo and Telnyx: whether their browser SDKs work on trial accounts.
  • Africa's Talking's official 2026 Kenya pricing.
